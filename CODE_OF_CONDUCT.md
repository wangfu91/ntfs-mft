# Code of conduct

We want contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels to be able
to participate safely and constructively.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and feedback; explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish personal information or disk
  captures belonging to others.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances, or use abusive
  language. Repeated unwanted contact and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces, as well as conduct while representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [Fu Wang](mailto:wangfu91@hotmail.com). Include
relevant links and context, sharing only the personal information necessary to
explain the concern. Reports will be handled as privately as possible; relevant
details may need to be shared to investigate or address the incident.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or ban participants depending on severity and repetition.
Enforcement should consider context and apply consistently. Retaliation against
someone who reports a concern in good faith is unacceptable.

To request reconsideration of a moderation decision, reply privately with any
additional context. This project currently has one maintainer and does not have
an independent appeal body.
